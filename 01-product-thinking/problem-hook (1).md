# Problem Hook & Value Proposition, Module 1

- **Scenario:** RouteLogic Velocity (B2B)

## Strategic crisis
If no action is taken within 12 months, RouteLogic risks losing enterprise customers to simpler, faster competitors, reducing its market share and competitive position.

## Moment of misery
Frontline coordinators rely on Excel spreadsheets, emails, and manual tracking to manage daily operations because the platform is too complex and time-consuming.

## Problem hook
RouteLogic's complexity is slowing down daily operations, frustrating users, and driving valuable enterprise customers toward competitors.

## Value proposition
For frontline coordinators, we will simplify workflows, reduce administrative tasks, and enable them to complete their work faster because RouteLogic needs to improve customer satisfaction and retention before more customers switch to competitors.

## Cold-read self-review
The strongest part is the clear business risk of losing enterprise customers to competitors. The weakest part is the generic term "users," which does not clearly identify frontline coordinators. I will address this by focusing on frontline coordinators and validating their specific workflow problems during the discovery phase.
